Privacy

Centurion Rental Hunt · last updated 15 October 2026

Centurion Rental Hunt is a private shortlist of rental listings that Dieter Olivier shares with a few invited people. This page explains what it stores when you sign in.

What we get from Google

When you click "Sign in with Google" we receive your name and email address, nothing else. We use them only to check that you're on the invite list and to show your first name next to the places you mark and the notes you write.

What we store

  • Your hearts, statuses, notes, and any phone numbers or emails you save for a listing, with your first name and the time. These live in a private GitHub repository only Dieter can open.
  • A sign-in session in your browser's local storage, so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it.

The listings themselves are public adverts collected from property websites.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside the people Dieter has invited.

Removing your data

Ask Dieter at dieteroli4@gmail.com and he'll remove you from the invite list and delete your notes.
